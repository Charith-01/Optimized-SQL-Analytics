# Analytical Findings

## Employee Analytics

- Support recorded the highest attrition rate at approximately 4.35%.
- Data recorded the highest average KPI at approximately 52.67.
- Engineering recorded the lowest average KPI at approximately 44.57.
- HR recorded the lowest average job satisfaction at approximately 2.70.
- Only 6 of the 200 employees had an attrition status of Yes.
- Employees who left had a higher average KPI than employees who stayed.
- The limited number of attrition records means the attrition comparison should be interpreted cautiously.

## Employee Risk Analysis

- 152 employees, or 76.00%, were categorized as Medium Risk.
- 23 employees, or 11.50%, were categorized as High Risk.
- 25 employees, or 12.50%, were categorized as Low Risk.
- These classifications were created using project-defined thresholds and are not official HR classifications.

## Recruitment Analytics

- QA recorded the highest hiring rate at approximately 42.11%.
- DS recorded a hiring rate of approximately 28.57%.
- SE recorded the lowest hiring rate at approximately 22.06%.
- Hired candidates had the highest average technical and interview scores.
- The highest composite candidate score was approximately 89.90.
- The composite score used a 40% technical, 30% interview, and 30% skill-match weighting.

## Limitations

- Each main dataset contains only approximately 200 records.
- Only six employee attrition records were available.
- The dataset does not include dates, limiting time-based analysis.
- Some source columns contain inconsistent categorical values.
- Analytical risk thresholds and composite-score weights were project assumptions.
